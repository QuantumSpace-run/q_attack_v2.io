# 🌊 DEEP SIGNAL

**A sequential multi-domain quantum decision exercise for professional military education and quantum-technology awareness.**

Deep Signal puts learners in the role of a joint task force commander facing a sophisticated adversary wielding an integrated quantum technology stack. Over five acts, players must diagnose symptoms, choose appropriate capabilities, and ultimately identify the adversary's true technology portfolio.

---

## 🎯 What Is This?

This is a **single-player browser-based game** that runs in any modern browser. No installation, no server, no accounts required.

Players work through five escalating acts covering:
- **Cyber/C2:** Harvest-now-decrypt-later cryptanalysis
- **Maritime/Undersea:** Magnetometric submarine tracking, gravimetric infrastructure detection
- **Air:** Quantum-optimized drone swarms
- **C2:** Post-quantum cryptography migration under pressure
- **Synthesis:** Identify the adversary's full convergence stack

Each act presents **six or more choices**, with consequences that carry forward. The game rewards early preparation (PQC migration takes years) and punishes buzzword-driven responses to misdiagnosed threats.

### Learning objectives

- Distinguish quantum computing (cryptanalysis, optimization), quantum sensing (magnetometry, gravimetry, seabed networks), and quantum communication (QKD) from conventional threats
- Understand why post-quantum crypto migration is a TODAY problem, not an emergency problem
- Recognize quantum convergence — an integrated system explaining multiple symptoms — vs. domain-by-domain thinking
- Practice indicator-based diagnosis before capability deployment

## 🕹️ Gameplay Overview

| Phase | Duration | Content |
|-------|----------|---------|
| Act I — The Whispering Net | 3–4 min | Cyber exfiltration with no breach |
| Act II — Silent Depths | 3–4 min | Submarine tracked without emissions |
| Act III — Ghost Horizon | 4–5 min | Adaptive swarm against sensor coverage |
| Act IV — The Broken Key | 3–4 min | Confirmed crypto break, strike order must be secured |
| Act V — The Stack | 2–3 min | Final synthesis — identify adversary's true portfolio |

**Scoring:** 12 base points + 1 continuity bonus for early PQC commitment.  
**Threat meter:** Red Advantage rises with failures, affecting campaign outcome.

## 🔧 Facilitator Guide

### Scoring thresholds

| Score | Assessment |
|-------|------------|
| 11–12 | Quantum fluency — convergence thinking mastered |
| 8–10 | Strong — mostly diagnostic, minor gaps |
| 5–7 | Developing — right instincts, uneven execution |
| 3–4 | Fragmented — domain-by-domain thinking dominates |
| 0–2 | Overmatched — stack not recognized |

### Debrief questions

1. Which act was hardest to diagnose, and why?
2. Where did you notice clues building toward Act V?
3. What changed when you realized this was ONE system, not three separate problems?
4. If you replayed with full knowledge, how would you start differently?
5. How does this translate to your organization's procurement timeline?

### Variant rounds

Edit the `ACTS` array in `index.html` to swap ground truths per act. Recommended changes:
- Make gravimetric survey the correct choice in Act II (strategic over tactical)
- Make quantum radar correct in Act III (drones actually stealth-coated this round)
- Disable synergy bonus for harder rounds

⚠️ **Warning:** When swapping truth keys, update the explanation text (`opt.r`) to avoid contradictions during debrief.
