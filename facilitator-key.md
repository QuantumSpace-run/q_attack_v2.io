## SCORING & SYNERGY

### Points awarded per act

| Performance | Points | Meaning |
|-------------|--------|---------|
| Decisive move | 2 | Correct diagnosis + correct tool |
| Partial success | 1 | Good instinct or strategic intelligence, but incomplete effect |
| Fail | 0 | Wrong model, wrong physics, or outdated thinking |

### Continuity Bonus (Act IV)

If player chose **PQC/forensics in Act I** AND chose **full PQC suite in Act IV** → **+1 point**  
**Teaching point:** Preparedness compounds; quantum readiness is a journey, not an emergency purchase

### Campaign Penalty (Final)

If **Act IV scored < 2** AND **RED ADVANTAGE ≥ HIGH** → **−1 point** from total  
**Teaching point:** Late-stage fixes come at higher operational cost

### Thresholds

| Score | Assessment |
|-------|------------|
| 11–12 | Quantum fluency — convergence thinking mastered |
| 8–10 | Strong — mostly diagnostic, minor gaps |
| 5–7 | Developing — right instincts, uneven execution |
| 3–4 | Fragmented — domain-by-domain thinking dominates |
| 0–2 | Overmatched — stack not recognized |

## RUNNING THE EXERCISE — CHECKLIST

### Before the session

| Task | Notes |
|------|-------|
| Print this key (or keep open privately) | Do NOT screen-share with participants |
| Test the game once | Verify scoring flow, continuity bonus triggers |
| Prepare debrief talking points | See next section for discussion guide |
| Set aside 20–30 minutes | Individual play is self-paced, but allow buffer |

### During play

| Observation | Intervention |
|-------------|--------------|
| Player hesitates > 5 minutes | Remind: "Diagnose the symptom, pick the tool. You can revise strategy later." |
| Multiple players, comparing notes | Encourage independence — variant rounds prevent cheating |
| Early finishers | Ask: "What would change if you knew the answer in advance?" |

### Debrief questions (10–15 minutes)

1. **Which act was hardest to diagnose, and why?** (Usually Act III — swarm feels like stealth, but it's optimization)
2. **Where did you notice the clues building toward Act V?** (Gravimetry network, forensics "no breach" finding)
3. **What changed when you realized this was ONE system, not three separate problems?** (Convergence mindset shift)
4. **If you replayed with full knowledge of the truth key, how would you start differently?** (PQC Day 1, not Day 4)
5. **How does this translate to your organization's procurement timeline?** (Migration = years, decision = seconds)

## VARIANT RULES (for repeat plays)

To create variant rounds, edit the `ACTS` array in `index.html`:

**Swap ground truths per act:**
- Act I: `v:"segment"` (treating as implant) as correct → teaches "sometimes the boring answer IS right"
- Act II: `v:"grav"` (gravimetry) as correct → rewards strategic over tactical thinking
- Act III: `v:"qradar"` (quantum radar) as correct → makes drones stealth-coated this round
- Act V: `v:"cyberonly"` as correct → makes the sub tracking purely computational

⚠️ **Important:** When you swap a ground truth, update the `opt.r` explanation text and `opt.hint` accordingly, otherwise players will spot the mismatch during debrief.

**Disable synergy bonus:** Remove the `if(act.id === 4 && opt.v === 'pqcsuite' && pqcEarly)` check from `commitChoice()`

**Increase difficulty:** Raise threat thresholds, add a requirement that Act V score must be ≥ 2 for any win
